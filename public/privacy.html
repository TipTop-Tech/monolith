Monolith ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is owned and operated by TipTop Technologies, located in Sunnyvale, California, United States.

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use the Monolith mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly to Us

  • Account Credentials: When creating an account, we collect your email address, username, and password. If you register via Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, we receive standard authorization tokens and the basic profile information you authorize (e.g., Apple relay email or Google account name/email).
  • Workout & Fitness Records: Custom workout routines, exercise names, logged sets, repetitions, weights lifted, workout timestamps, rest intervals, and routine notes.
  • App Preferences: Selected weight unit (LB or KG), custom rest duration, visual skins/themes, sound and haptic feedback toggles, and Live Activity status.
  • Support Communications: Inquiries sent to our support team to diagnose issues and answer questions.

B. Information Processed Automatically

  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, unique application installation ID, IP address, and network connection status.
  • Local Storage: Fast on-device SQLite database and preferences saved securely within your device's isolated application sandbox.
  • Performance Diagnostics: Anonymous crash reports and web performance indicators (via Vercel Web Analytics).

C. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All payments and subscriptions are processed directly through Apple App Store (StoreKit). TipTop Technologies never collects, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses. We receive an anonymized transaction identifier and entitlement status via RevenueCat to unlock premium features and restore purchases.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To log workouts, track personal records, calculate training volume, and present exercise guides.
  • To seamlessly synchronize your routines and workout history between local device storage and our cloud backend.
  • To power active workout timers on the iOS Lock Screen and Dynamic Island via iOS Live Activities.
  • To authenticate your identity, prevent fraud, and manage account sessions.
  • To verify active subscription status and enable seamless purchase restoration.
  • To diagnose software defects and optimize application responsiveness.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit between Monolith and remote servers. On your device, your workout records are kept inside the secure local application sandbox protected by iOS operating system encryption. Remote cloud servers are housed in enterprise-grade data centers with strict access controls and encryption at rest.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In-App Account Deletion (Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) Compliant):
You can delete your account and all associated data directly within Monolith at any time by navigating to Account → Delete Account. Confirming deletion immediately wipes your local workout records and triggers permanent erasure of your cloud account and workout history from our servers.

You may also request account deletion by emailing us at ishaankumarb65@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered account email. Verified requests will be processed within 30 days.

6. Minimum Age Policy

Monolith is strictly intended for individuals 16 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If we become aware that an individual under 16 has registered an account, we will immediately delete their information. Parents or guardians may contact us at ishaankumarb65@gmail.com.

7. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have specific rights under the CCPA/CPRA:

  • Right to Know / Access: Request details of personal information collected in the past 12 months.
  • Right to Deletion: Request permanent erasure of your personal records.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do not sell your personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Non-Discrimination: We never discriminate against users for exercising privacy rights.

8. European Union (GDPR) & UK Privacy Rights

Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland have rights under GDPR including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to lodge complaints with local Data Protection Authorities.
